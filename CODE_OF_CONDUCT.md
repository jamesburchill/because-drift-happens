# Code of Conduct

Because Drift Happens is a public framework for serious technical and operational discussion. Contributors are expected to participate with professionalism, clarity, and respect.

## Expected Conduct

- Treat contributors, maintainers, and readers with respect.
- Critique ideas, definitions, and patterns directly without personal attacks.
- Prefer evidence, examples, and clear reasoning over rhetorical force.
- Make uncertainty explicit when a claim is exploratory.
- Keep discussions relevant to the framework.

## Unacceptable Conduct

- Harassment, abuse, threats, or discriminatory language.
- Personal attacks or repeated bad-faith argument.
- Publishing private, confidential, or client material.
- Vendor spam or promotional content.
- Deliberate disruption of issue, pull request, or RFC discussions.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, close, or remove contributions that do not meet these standards. Repeated or serious violations may result in blocked participation.
