# Diagram Placeholders

The following diagrams are planned placeholders. Each should be developed only when the underlying concept is clear enough to represent visually.

## Drift Loop

A diagram showing how intent, context, execution, outcome, feedback, and adaptation can reinforce or correct drift.

## Drift Triplet Scope View

A nested diagram showing drift, autonomous drift, and federated drift as increasingly broad scopes.

## Drift Triplet Escalation View

A stacked diagram showing drift escalating from small deviation to autonomous divergence to federated systemic risk.

## Governance Triplet View

A paired response diagram showing awareness revealing drift, alignment reducing drift, and coordination containing drift.

## Control Plane Above Agents

A diagram showing agents and workflows in the execution layer, with a control plane above them for observability, policy checks, authority resolution, intervention, and auditability.

## Governance Checkpoint

A flow showing a proposed action entering a checkpoint, being evaluated against policy and authority, then approved, held, vetoed, or escalated.

## Federation Coherence Boundary

A diagram showing multiple autonomous actors inside and across coherence boundaries, with shared signals and escalation paths.

## Intervention Ladder

A diagram showing escalating intervention options: observe, warn, hold, require review, veto, contain, roll back, and escalate.
